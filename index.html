Calculation method

Hydraulic design manual — quick reference

For steady, uniform open-channel flow, the Manning equation estimates conveyance:

Q = (1/n) A R2/3 S1/2

where Q is discharge, n is Manning roughness, A is flow area, R = A/P is hydraulic radius, P is wetted perimeter, and S is energy/longitudinal slope expressed as a decimal. Actual mean velocity is checked separately as V = Q/A against the user-entered maximum.

For a rectangular section: A = by and P = b + 2y. For a trapezoidal section with side slope z horizontal to 1 vertical: A = by + zy² and P = b + 2y√(1+z²). The search selects a candidate with Manning capacity at least Q and actual velocity no greater than Vmax.

Important: NSCP 2015 is not, by itself, a complete hydraulic drainage canal design manual. Use applicable local DPWH drainage/hydraulic criteria and site-specific hydrology. Final reinforced-concrete design must be checked against applicable structural codes and engineering standards.

Limitations

Engineering review required

This standalone tool is for preliminary planning and quantity estimation, not a permit-ready or construction-ready design. Confirm design storm and catchment hydrology, freeboard criteria, permissible velocity and erosion protection, tailwater/backwater, transitions, bends, inlet/outlet control, sediment, debris, scour, geotechnical conditions, uplift, external earth/water pressure, traffic/surcharge loads, durability/exposure, crack control, concrete cover, bar development and splices, construction joints, and local agency requirements. A licensed civil/structural engineer should verify the final design.
